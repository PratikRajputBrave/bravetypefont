# Privacy Policy - BraveType - Font Manager Tool

**Brave Studios** is committed to absolute user privacy.

## Privacy Principles

1. **100% Local Application**: BraveType runs entirely offline on your local computer.
2. **Zero Telemetry or Analytics**: We do not collect, track, store, or transmit any analytics, usage statistics, or telemetry.
3. **No Network Traffic**: BraveType makes no external network requests. All font scanning, parsing, caching, and store management occurs locally inside Electron's `userData` directory.
4. **No Personal Data Collection**: Your font files, collections, favorites, and settings never leave your machine.
